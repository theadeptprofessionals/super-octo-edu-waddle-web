# Schoolon Africa — Web Landing Page & Early Access Portal

> **A Digital Secondary School Built for African Students**  
> Empowering JSS1–SS3 students across Africa to continue their education no matter the circumstance with NERDC-aligned curriculum, AI dictionary in 50+ local languages, real 1-on-1 graduate mentors, and physical assessments.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Multi-Stakeholder Waitlist Portal**: Tailored onboarding workflows for **Students**, **Parents**, **Mentors**, and **Investors / Partners** with instant validation and dynamic fields.
* **Live Community Counter**: Real-time waitlist counter connected to Supabase with secure PostgreSQL RPC aggregation (`get_waitlist_count`) and local fallback.
* **Interactive AI Local Language Dictionary**: Live interactive demo for English STEM concepts translated into Yoruba, Hausa, and Igbo with voice audio indication.
* **No Student Left Behind Impact Section**: Highlighting continuity of learning for teen mothers, bereaved students, and remote learners with 100% accredited certification pathways.
* **Mobile-First Learning Showcase**: Realistic smartphone preview of offline video downloads, low-bandwidth mode, and CBT past question preparation.
* **Embedded Legal Modals**: Instant, accessible Privacy Policy and Terms of Service modal viewer without navigating away from the page.
